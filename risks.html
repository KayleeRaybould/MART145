Major Health Risks

Kratom can affect the body in different ways depending on the amount used and the person using it. Although kratom comes from a plant, natural does not always mean something is safe.

Kratom plant. Image: Wikimedia Commons, CC0.

Some reported risks include liver toxicity, seizures, dependence, withdrawal, and contamination in unregulated products. People should be cautious and seek reliable information before using kratom.

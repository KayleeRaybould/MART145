What is Kratom?

Kratom is a plant-based substance derived from the leaves of the Mitragyna speciosa tree, which grows naturally in Southeast Asia. It has been used traditionally for its stimulant and pain-relieving properties, but in recent years it has become widely available in the United States through smoke shops, gas stations, and online retailers.

The active compounds in kratom, mitragynine and 7-hydroxymitragynine, interact with opioid receptors in the brain. This means kratom can produce effects similar to opioids, including euphoria, pain relief, and sedation. However, it can also cause dependence, withdrawal, and serious health risks.
